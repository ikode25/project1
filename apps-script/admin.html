Welcome to E-Report System
Let's walk you through the key features in just a few steps.
1
Dashboard Overview
Your Dashboard shows a live summary — total students, classes, recent activities, academic performance charts and term trends. It's your command center for the whole school.
Tip: The top bar shows the active academic year and term. You can switch sessions anytime from the header dropdowns.
2
System Settings
Go to Settings to configure your school name, logo, grading system, SMS alerts, report templates, and typography. All changes save to the cloud and sync instantly.
Tip: Upload your school logo and stamp under Settings → School Info so they appear on all generated report cards.
3
Student Records & Reports
Add and manage students under Students. Teachers enter SBA and exam scores; the system auto-calculates grades, positions, and generates printable report cards for each student.
Tip: Use Bulk Print to generate report cards for an entire class in one click.
4
Teachers & Portals
Add teachers in Manage Teachers. Each teacher logs in with their own credentials and can only access their assigned class. They can enter scores, view their profile, and access tutorials.
Tip: You can set the default teacher password from Settings → Security at any time.
5
SMS & WhatsApp Alerts
Send SMS or WhatsApp messages to parents directly from the system. Configure your Arkesel SMS API key in Settings → SMS API to enable bulk parent notifications.
Tip: Visit Settings → Video Tutorial to watch a full walkthrough video once the developer adds the tutorial link.
1
Your Teacher Dashboard
Welcome! Your dashboard shows your assigned class, recent score entries, and a quick summary of your students. Everything here is specific to your class only.
Tip: Your username and class assignment were set by the Admin. Contact them if you need to update your credentials.
2
Entering SBA & Exam Scores
Go to Score Entry (or use the full-screen score editor) to enter Class Assessment (SBA) and End-of-Term Exam scores for each student. The system calculates totals automatically.
Tip: Press Tab to move between cells quickly when entering scores.
3
Student Reports & Profile
You can view and preview report cards for your students. Update your profile photo and signature from the Settings tab — your signature will appear on every report card you generate.
Tip: Check Settings → Video Tutorial to watch a guided video on how to use the full system efficiently.
