# Golden Clippers — Barber & Salon Enterprise Management System

A complete, production-ready **Barber & Salon management system for Ghana**, built entirely on **Google Apps Script** with a bound **Google Sheet** as the database. It ships as two files:

- **`Code.gs`** — all backend logic: Google Sheets data access, authentication, booking, POS, inventory, reports, SMS/Email notifications.
- **`index.html`** — the entire front-end: public marketing/booking website **and** the secured admin/staff dashboard, in one responsive, low-bandwidth-friendly page.

## Features

### Public website
- Mobile-first hero carousel, fully admin-editable (images, titles, buttons)
- Live services & price list (GH₵) grouped by category, pulled from the sheet
- Staff/team showcase, each stylist showing their working days
- **Photo gallery** of work samples with a lightbox viewer, fully admin-managed. **Every service's photo is click-to-enlarge too** — the same full-screen lightbox opens from the Services & Price List so a customer can see exactly what they're booking
- Built for a single shop — no branch selector, no "which location" step anywhere on the site
- A friendly **greeting banner** ("Good afternoon! Welcome to…") just below the navbar

Ghana phone number validation throughout (`0XXXXXXXXX` or `+233XXXXXXXXX`)
- **Pay-at-booking (optional)** — at checkout the customer picks from whichever payment methods the Owner has switched on in Settings (Cash at Front Desk, MTN MoMo, Vodafone Cash, Telecel Cash, AirtelTigo Money, Bank Transfer — see **Accepted Payment Methods** below). Cash needs nothing further; any mobile money/bank option shows the Owner's number/name or bank details with a one-tap **Copy** button next to the number, and requires the customer to upload a screenshot of their payment as proof before they can confirm. **Each mobile money network has its own number** (MTN MoMo, Vodafone Cash, Telecel Cash, and AirtelTigo Money are genuinely different wallets in Ghana — a customer can't send Vodafone Cash to an MTN number), and a method only ever shows to customers once the Owner has actually filled in its number/account — an unconfigured network or bank account is automatically hidden even if switched on, so no one is offered a way to pay that doesn't actually work. - **"Send Us a Message" contact form** on the public site — emails straight to the business's Contact Email (via `MailApp`), no admin login needed
- Customer reviews display **plus a public "Leave a Review" star-rating form**
- **"My Bookings"** — search by the phone number you booked with, *or* by your booking reference code; results show as a **Kanban board grouped by status** (Pending/Confirmed/Completed/No-show/Cancelled), with a Cancel button on any upcoming booking

- A floating **WhatsApp button** (pre-filled "I'd like to book an appointment" message) and a floating **chat-bubble FAQ assistant** that answers from your live services/prices/hours/contact data — a fast rule-based assistant, not a paid generative AI, so it works with no API key or ongoing cost
- A small hand-built inline-SVG icon set throughout (no emoji, no icon-font download)
- **Visual refresh**: bolder/larger heading scale, an accent-underlined section-title treatment, deeper card shadows with a lift-on-hover on service/staff/review cards, a bigger and more legible hero with a stronger text-shadow and underline-on-hover nav links, a more prominent footer with an accent top border, and smooth-scrolling anchor navigation — aimed at reading as a modern, professional small-business site rather than a bare form-driven page

This app runs for exactly one shop. The `Branches` sheet still exists — its columns are how the shop's own name, location, phone, and hours are stored — but it always holds exactly one row, edited from Dashboard → **Branding & Theme** alongside the rest of the business's settings, never a list to add to or delete from. `Staff.WorkDays` is a comma-separated list of weekday abbreviations (e.g. `Mon,Tue,Wed,Thu,Fri,Sat`) that drives each stylist's permanent "Available/Not Available" status and the calendar in the public booking wizard; a row in `StaffLeave` adds a one-off exception on top of that — a specific date that one stylist is out, without touching their regular schedule. `Branches.WeeklyHours` (JSON shape `{ Sun: { open, is24, start, end }, Mon: {...}, … }`, edited from **Branding & Theme**) is the single source of truth for the booking wizard's time slots, the Manage Slots grid, the public "Working Hours" display, and the live Open Now/Closed Now badge. `Branches.OpeningHours` is never typed by hand — it's auto-derived from `WeeklyHours` (e.g. "Mon-Sat 8:00 AM – 7:00 PM, Sun 12:00 PM – 5:00 PM") the moment settings are saved, so there's only one place that can go out of sync. `Settings` also stores `SlotIntervalMinutes`, `ActivePaymentMethods` (comma-separated subset of the six payment methods actually offered at checkout), `SocialFacebook`/`SocialInstagram`/`SocialTwitter`/`SocialTiktok`/`SocialYoutube`, `FontFamily` (the site font), and a separate number/account-name pair per payment method — `MomoNumber`/`MomoName` (MTN MoMo), `VodafoneCashNumber`/`VodafoneCashName`, `TelecelCashNumber`/`TelecelCashName`, `AirtelTigoMoneyNumber`/`AirtelTigoMoneyName`, and `BankName`/`BankAccountName`/`BankAccountNumber`. `Users.FullName` is captured so an Owner can tell who a pending self-registered account actually belongs to before approving it, and now doubles as the name used in the admin greeting banner. A row in `BlockedSlots` closes one time slot on one date — managed from the **Manage Slots** admin page. `Appointments.PaymentMethod` is one of `Cash`/`MTN MoMo`/`Vodafone Cash`/`Telecel Cash`/`AirtelTigo Money`/`Bank Transfer`; `PaymentStatus` is `Pay at Shop` (cash), `Awaiting Payment` (chose to pay now but hasn't uploaded proof yet), `Pending Verification` (proof uploaded, awaiting admin review), or `Paid` (admin verified it); `PaymentProofURL` is the customer's uploaded screenshot, stored in the same Drive folder as other site images. A row in `Visits` is one visitor-day (deduped by `VisitorKey` + `Date`), used only for the Overview visitor counters. Every other sheet's `BranchID` column is an internal reference to that one shop — it's how Services/Staff/Products/Expenses/Appointments/Sales/BlockedSlots/Gallery/Users rows tie back to it, but nothing in the admin UI ever asks you to choose one.

The schema self-heals: if a sheet or column above is missing from an already-deployed spreadsheet (e.g. you update to a newer version of this app that added a column), the next page load automatically creates/fixes it — no manual re-run of `setupSheets()` needed.

Sample data (one shop, 10 services, 5 staff with working days, 4 products, 5 customers, 8 reviews, 2 hero slides, 6 gallery photos) is seeded automatically on first run so the app is immediately demoable.

## Deployment

1. **Create the Google Sheet.** Go to [sheets.google.com](https://sheets.google.com), create a new blank spreadsheet (this will be your database).
2. **Open the Apps Script editor.** In the Sheet, go to `Extensions → Apps Script`.
3. **Add the code.**
   - Rename the default `Code.gs` file (or paste over it) with the contents of `Code.gs` from this repo.
   - Click the `+` next to Files → `HTML` → name it exactly `index` → paste in the contents of `index.html`.
4. **Run setup once.** In the Apps Script editor toolbar, select the `setupSheets` function from the function dropdown and click **Run**. Grant the requested permissions (Sheets, Drive, Gmail/MailApp, external requests for SMS). This creates every tab with headers and seeds sample data, including a default admin login (see below).
5. **Deploy as a Web App.**
   - Click **Deploy → New deployment**.
   - Select type **Web app**.
   - Set **Execute as**: `Me`.
   - Set **Who has access**: `Anyone` (so customers can reach the public booking page without logging in).
   - Click **Deploy**, authorize, and copy the **Web app URL** — this is your live site.
6. **(Optional) Update an existing deployment.** After making changes, use **Deploy → Manage deployments → Edit → New version** so the live URL picks up your changes.
7. **(Optional) Daily appointment reminders.** In the Apps Script editor, go to `Triggers` (clock icon) → `Add Trigger` → function `sendUpcomingAppointmentReminders`, time-driven, run daily (e.g. every day 8am–9am, Africa/Accra time zone) to automatically SMS/email customers about tomorrow's appointment.

## Notes on architecture
- Every price shown to a customer or used in a sale is resolved **server-side** from the `Services`/`Products` sheets — the client only ever sends IDs and quantities, preventing price tampering.
- All `google.script.run` calls go through a single `api()` wrapper with a visible loading bar and toast-based error handling — no silent failures.
- Sessions are stateless server tokens (`CacheService`, 6-hour TTL) — no passwords are ever kept in the browser beyond the login form submission.
- The whole front end is one HTML file with no external JS/CSS framework, keeping the public page light for customers on mobile data in Ghana.
- **Every sheet column is forced to Plain Text format** (`setNumberFormat('@')`) at setup time. Without this, Google Sheets silently "smart-formats" values that look like numbers or times as you write them — a phone number like `0247123456` loses its leading zero, and a time slot like `09:00` gets converted into an actual time value — which then breaks exact-match lookups (phone number search, slot-availability checks) elsewhere in the app.
- **Uploaded photos/logos are delivered as embedded image data, not hotlinked or fetched as a separate resource at all.** Four things were tried across earlier updates to get an uploaded photo to display reliably: three different Drive hotlink URL formats (`uc?export=view`, `drive.google.com/thumbnail`, `lh3.googleusercontent.com/d/...`), and then routing through this app's own `doGet(e)` as a `?img=<fileId>` link. Every one of them asks the browser to fetch the image as its own separate HTTP request — and it turns out an `<img>` tag inside this app's own HtmlService-sandboxed page, fetching from any of those (including the app's own address), runs into Google's redirect/consent handling for that flow unpredictably. That's why photos kept failing to preview — first just in Safari, then (once routed through the app's own `?img=` link) everywhere at once. The actual fix: stop asking the browser to fetch images as separate resources. Every endpoint that hands data to the browser (`getPublicData`, `getServices`, `getStaff`, `getProducts`, `getGallery`, `getHeroSlides`, `getSettings`, `updateSettings`) now converts each Drive-backed image reference into a `data:` URI — the real image bytes, read server-side and embedded directly in the same `google.script.run` response already carrying everything else that page needs (`imageUrlToDataUri_()`/`withImageDataUris_()`). That's the one delivery path already proven to work identically in every browser, because the rest of the app's data already relies on it — no external host, no redirect, no separate fetch for the browser to handle differently. This recognizes and converts image references saved under any of the three prior URL formats too, so already-uploaded photos display correctly without being re-uploaded — nothing to migrate, since the small reference stored in the sheet doesn't change, only how it's read. The one place that still needs a real, independently-fetchable link is HTML email (a mail client fetches images itself, with no `google.script.run` channel available) — the greeting/reminder emails' logo intentionally keeps using the raw stored URL for that reason, via `doGet`'s `?img=` handler, which remains in place as a plain top-level "open this file" link (also used for the admin's "View Proof" payment-screenshot link, which needs the same thing).
- **The upload folder is created up front, not lazily.** `SalonSystem_Uploads` — the one Drive folder every uploaded photo lands in (logo, staff, services, products, gallery, hero slides, payment proofs) — is now created during `setupSheets()` itself rather than waiting for the first upload to trigger it. Dashboard → Branding & Theme has a new "View Uploads Folder in Drive" link next to the logo picker for the Owner to open it directly.
- **`SETUP_VERSION` — a durable, versioned migration gate.** Earlier, the Plain Text fix above (and any future one like it) only actually ran against a spreadsheet whose sheet *headers* didn't already match — so on a spreadsheet created before the fix existed, it silently never took effect even after redeploying newer code, because the headers already matched and the check short-circuited. `ensureSetup_()` now also compares a `setupVersion` stored in `PropertiesService` (which survives forever, unlike the 1-hour `CacheService` flag) against the in-code `SETUP_VERSION` constant, and forces `setupSheets()` to run at least once whenever it's behind — guaranteeing every such fix actually reaches every existing spreadsheet, not just brand-new ones. Bump `SETUP_VERSION` any time you add a fix here that needs to be re-applied to already-deployed spreadsheets.
- **One-time data repair on that same migration**: `repairPhoneColumns_()` restores a phone number that lost its leading zero to a 9-digit number before the Plain Text fix took effect (any 9-digit value in a Phone column is unambiguously missing that zero), and `repairTimeSlotColumns_()` fixes a `TimeSlot` value that got silently stored as a Time value instead of a plain "HH:mm" string (the same root cause — a value that *looks* like a time gets auto-formatted before Plain Text is in effect). The latter was the actual cause of two separately-reported symptoms: a slot closed in **Manage Slots** not staying visually marked closed after a reload, and (in principle) availability math being thrown off — because the stored TimeSlot could no longer exact-match the "HH:mm" keys the rest of the app looks it up by. Both repairs are idempotent and run automatically on the next page load after this update; they can't recover a booking's *other* fields, only its Phone/TimeSlot values.
- The one-time schema self-heal check (`ensureSetup_`, described above) is cached for an hour via `CacheService` rather than running on every page load — the earlier version re-verified all 13+ sheet headers on every single request, which was the single biggest cause of the app feeling slow to open.
- **`updateSettings()` now reads and writes the Settings sheet once, not once per key.** Saving ~30 settings at once used to mean ~30+ separate `getValues()`/`setValues()` round trips (one per key, via the same generic `findRowIndexById_`/`updateById_` helpers every other sheet uses) — that serial chain of small Sheets API calls was the actual cause of "Save All Settings" feeling slow. It now reads the whole (small) sheet once, updates everything in memory, and writes it back in one or two calls total.
- **Manage Slots trusts the toggle call's own response, not a re-fetch.** Toggling a slot used to call `toggleBlockedSlot` and then immediately re-fetch the whole day's grid with `getSlotsForAdmin` to reconcile the UI. That extra read was removed — the mutation's own response already says exactly what was just written, so using it directly is both faster (one round trip instead of two) and immune to a slot ever appearing to silently revert to open after a click.
- **One weekly-hours source of truth drives everything time-related.** `getEffectiveWeeklyHours_()` reads the shop's own `Branches.WeeklyHours` (set from Branding & Theme), and `getAvailableSlots()` (public booking) and `getSlotsForAdmin()` (Manage Slots) both derive that date's start/end minute window — and whether the day is bookable at all — from it, instead of a flat all-week start/end hour. A day marked closed is gated out before staff schedules (or a stylist's `StaffLeave`) are even considered, both in what's offered and in `createAppointment()`'s own server-side re-validation (`isSlotAvailable_`), so the public wizard, Manage Slots, and the actual booking guard can never disagree about whether a given day is open.
- Apps Script + Google Sheets is a real, live database, not an in-memory cache — every save and most page loads involve at least one live round trip to Sheets, so some latency (typically under a couple of seconds) is inherent to this platform and can't be fully eliminated. Buttons across the app now give immediate visual feedback (disabled + "Saving…" state, optimistic UI on Manage Slots, confirmation toasts) so actions register clearly even while that round trip is in flight.
- **Stale-while-revalidate (SWR) caching on the public site.** The very first thing a visitor's browser does used to be wait on a live `getPublicData` round trip before anything appeared. Now, if a copy of that data is sitting in `localStorage` from the last 5 minutes, the whole public site (services, staff, gallery, videos, reviews, hero, theme…) paints from it **instantly**, while a fresh copy is fetched silently in the background and swapped in — without resetting the hero slider or an in-progress booking wizard if the visitor has already started one. This is scoped to the public site's initial load, the single biggest lever for perceived speed since every visitor hits it; it is not (yet) applied to every admin list.
- **Multi-branch support has been removed entirely — this app now runs for exactly one shop, full stop.** There used to be a `Settings.HasBranches` toggle switching between a multi-location mode (an admin-managed list of branches, an owner branch switcher in the topbar, a branch picker in every admin modal) and a single-shop mode. That toggle, the Branches list page, the "Add Branch"/"Delete Branch" buttons, and the topbar branch switcher are all gone, backed by new `getShopInfo`/`saveShopInfo` endpoints (`getBranches`/`saveBranch`/`deleteBranch` no longer exist). A one-time migration (`mergeToSingleBranch_()`, part of the same `SETUP_VERSION`-gated setup pass) collapses an already-deployed spreadsheet's `Branches` sheet down to its first row — reassigning every Service/Staff/Product/Expense/Appointment/Sale/BlockedSlot/Gallery/User row that pointed at any other branch over to the surviving one first, so nothing is silently orphaned — before deleting the extra rows. `branchArg()`/`showBranchPicker_()`/`branchSelectHtml()`/`branchSelectValue()` — the helpers every branch-scoped admin modal (Appointments, Services, Staff, Products, Expenses, Gallery, Manage Slots, Users) already read from — still exist, but now always resolve to that one shop with no picker ever rendered, since there is structurally nothing else to pick from.
- **Shop Info folded into Branding & Theme, and the Booking settings tab is gone.** What was briefly its own "Shop Info" sidebar page (immediately after removing multi-branch support) turned out to be one more place competing for attention next to the rest of the business's settings, for data that's really part of the same "who is this business" picture as its name and logo. Dashboard → **Branding & Theme → Branding** now holds Business Name/Tagline/Logo, a **Shop Info** section (location, phone, slot interval), and the **Working Hours** editor, all in one tab — there's no separate Shop Info page anymore, and the Booking tab (which had shrunk to just the slot interval and a pointer to Shop Info once its working-hours editor moved out) is gone too, folded into the same place. One **Save All Settings** button saves both the `Settings` sheet (`updateSettings`) and the shop's own `Branches` row (`saveShopInfo`) together in parallel, so there's still only one save action on the page even though it now writes to two sheets.
- **Service Categories are now admin-managed, not free-typed per service.** Dashboard → Services previously let each service's "Category" be typed by hand, so "Haircut" and "Haircuts" (or a stray trailing space) would silently split what should have been one category in the public site's service filter. The Services admin page now opens with a "Service Categories" panel — an editable chip list stored in `Settings.ServiceCategories` (comma-separated) — and the Add/Edit Service modal offers a `<select>` populated from that list instead of a text box. Removing a category from the list doesn't touch services already saved under it; an existing service's category is appended to the dropdown automatically if it's ever out of sync with the current list, so old data is never silently orphaned or blanked.
- **Public site sections can be switched off from Settings.** Dashboard → Branding & Theme now has two toggles — "Greeting banner" and "Our Team section" (`Settings.ShowGreetingBanner`/`ShowTeamSection`, default `'Y'`) — for Owners who don't want a personalized greeting strip or a staff-photos section on their public page. Turning "Our Team" off also removes its nav link, so there's no dead link pointing at a hidden section.
- **The "Open Now"/"Closed Now" badge is styled as a proper status tag with a live pulse.** It's a pill with a colored dot instead of plain text — green with a soft pulsing ring animation when open (so it visibly reads as "live," not static text), red and still when closed.
- **The public site has its own reload button**, next to the mobile menu icon in the top nav (the admin dashboard already had one). It re-fetches `getPublicData` and gives a brief spin + confirmation toast, for a customer who wants to double-check live availability/pricing without a full page refresh.
- **The admin notifications dropdown no longer overflows off-screen on narrow phones.** It's positioned with `right: 0` relative to the bell button itself, which works fine on desktop but, on a narrow viewport where the bell sits close to the screen's left edge, a fixed 300px-wide panel growing leftward from that anchor could run out of screen — clipping its own content off the left edge instead of shifting to fit. Below 860px wide it now anchors to the viewport instead of the bell button (`position: fixed` with `left`/`right` insets, matching the same top offset already used by the mobile help-chat panel in this topbar), so it always fits fully on screen regardless of exactly where the bell button lands in the topbar's wrapped layout.
- **The admin dashboard no longer loads the public site's data just to open.** Embedding uploaded images as `data:` URIs (above) made every admin page that shows photos noticeably slower to open, because `getPublicData()` — which the app's boot sequence called unconditionally, even when heading straight to `#admin` — was fetching and base64-encoding every service, staff, gallery, and hero-slide photo before the dashboard could even appear. The boot sequence now checks for an existing admin session before deciding what to load: heading to `#admin` with a valid token skips `getPublicData()` entirely and fetches only `getSettings`+`getShopInfo` (one image at most — the logo) — everything the dashboard shell actually needs to render immediately, with each individual admin page (Services, Staff, Gallery, etc.) still fetching its own data, images included, only once that page is actually opened. The one admin function that had come to depend on the public payload for convenience — the "New Appointment" modal's service/staff dropdowns — now fetches from the proper authenticated `getServices`/`getStaff` endpoints instead, so it no longer requires the public site's data to have loaded at all.
- **Uploaded images are now cached, so a repeat page load doesn't re-fetch and re-encode every photo from Drive again.** `imageUrlToDataUri_()` checks `CacheService` (keyed by the Drive file ID) before doing the live fetch+base64-encode, and stores the result for 6 hours after computing it — shared across every admin and visitor hitting the script, not just the browser that first triggered it. Images under Google Cache's 100KB-per-value limit (the large majority of typical business photos) get the full benefit; a rare larger photo just isn't cached and keeps paying the live-fetch cost on every load, without breaking anything. Since a fresh upload always creates a brand-new Drive file (never overwrites one), a cached entry can never go stale from a re-upload.
- **Fixed: clicking a time slot in Manage Slots did nothing.** `toggleSlotAdmin()` read the branch ID directly from a `#slotBranch` dropdown element that no longer exists now that branch pickers are permanently hidden (single-shop mode) — `document.getElementById('slotBranch').value` threw on a null element and silently aborted the click with no visible error. It now resolves the branch the same safe way every other branch-scoped action does (`branchSelectValue('slotBranch') || branchArg()`), which was already correct in `loadSlotsAdmin()` right next to it — this one call site had been missed when branch pickers were removed.
- **Contact section redesigned** to match the card-driven, two-tone treatment used by higher-end business sites, instead of a plain stacked list next to a map. A dark `.contact-info-bar` presents Call/WhatsApp/Email/Hours as four elevated cards with gold-accented icons (matching the site's own theme colors), followed by the social icon row, then a `.contact-split` pairing the Google Map with the "Send Us a Message" form as two matched elevated cards. The section heading now uses the same centered `.section-title` treatment (with the accent underline) as every other section on the page, which it had been missing.
- **The public site's dead branch selector is gone — and with it, a visible flash on every page load.** A "All Branches" dropdown above the hero (left over from before multi-branch support was removed) had nothing left to ever show but that one option, since `branches.length` is always 1 now — but it was still in the static HTML with no default-hidden state, so it visibly flashed on screen for a moment on every load before JavaScript got around to hiding it. It's deleted outright now, along with `renderBranchSelector()` and the now-pointless branch-filter parameter `renderServices()`/`renderStaff()` had carried since it could only ever be empty.
- **Section background alternation is now a clean, consistent checkerboard.** Three sections in a row (Team, Videos, Booking) had ended up sharing the same background with no visual separation between them, breaking the alternating light/white rhythm every other section already had. Booking and Reviews now carry the `.alt` (white) background and My Bookings dropped it, restoring a strict alternation down the whole page.
- **A more polished loading experience, sitewide.** The top progress bar used to jump straight to a static 70% width and hold there for the whole request, snapping to 0 the instant it finished — now it eases toward 90% over a few seconds with a subtle shimmer animation (a believable sense of progress with no real progress to report), then snaps to 100% and fades out the moment the response actually lands. Every plain "Loading…" text placeholder across the admin dashboard (Appointments, Customers, Overview, Manage Slots, Income & Expenses, Staff time-off, SMS) is replaced by a small spinning-ring + label component (`loadingHtml()`), instead of static text sitting in an empty panel.
- **POS now shows which items are already in the cart.** Clicking a service/product card in Point of Sale added it to the cart, but the card itself gave no visual sign it had been picked — clicking the same item repeatedly (to bump quantity) meant reading the cart list on the other side of the screen to check. Every card now gets an accent-colored border and a quantity badge the moment it's in the cart, updated in place (`updatePosItemSelection()`) on every add/remove without rebuilding the whole grid, so the search filter and scroll position never reset.
- **Found the actual cause of "every page is slow" (not just image-heavy ones): images were being fetched from Drive completely sequentially, one at a time, within a single request.** Embedding photos as `data:` URIs (to fix the Safari preview issue) meant every page with photos now had to read and base64-encode each one — but the original implementation called `DriveApp.getFileById(id).getBlob()` in a plain loop, so a page with a dozen photos meant a dozen live Drive round trips back to back before the response could even finish assembling. CacheService (added the round after that) only helped once an image had been *seen before*; the very first load of any page, and every load right after a redeploy with an empty cache, still paid that full serial cost — which is exactly why it showed up as "all pages," worst right after redeploying, and slow enough to feel like a hang. `batchResolveImageUrls_()` replaces the per-image loop: every still-uncached image needed for a response is now fetched in one `UrlFetchApp.fetchAll()` call, which Apps Script executes with real concurrency instead of one at a time — N sequential round trips become effectively one. `getPublicData()` (the single worst offender, since it touches the logo, every service, every staff member, every hero slide, and every gallery photo in one call) batches all of those together into one combined fetch rather than five separate ones. `imageUrlToDataUri_()` and `withImageDataUris_()` keep their exact same signatures, so every existing caller across the file gets this for free with no other changes needed.
