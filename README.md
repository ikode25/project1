# Golden Clippers — Barber & Salon Enterprise Management System

A complete, production-ready **Barber & Salon management system for Ghana**, built entirely on **Google Apps Script** with a bound **Google Sheet** as the database. It ships as two files:

- **`Code.gs`** — all backend logic: Google Sheets data access, authentication, booking, POS, inventory, reports, SMS/Email notifications.
- **`index.html`** — the entire front-end: public marketing/booking website **and** the secured admin/staff dashboard, in one responsive, low-bandwidth-friendly page.

## Features

### Public website
- Mobile-first hero carousel, fully admin-editable (images, titles, buttons)
- Live services & price list (GH₵) grouped by category, pulled from the sheet
- Staff/team showcase, each stylist showing their working days
- **Photo gallery** of work samples with a lightbox viewer, fully admin-managed. **Every service's photo is click-to-enlarge too** — the same full-screen lightbox opens from the Services & Price List so a customer can see exactly what they're booking
- Multi-branch selector — or a simple single-shop layout if the Owner turns off "multiple branches" in Settings (not every barbershop has more than one location). Single-shop mode is fully enforced server-side: the public site and booking wizard only ever see one branch, no matter how many branch rows still exist behind the scenes (e.g. leftover sample data) — nothing is deleted, the Owner just switches back on to see them all again
- A friendly **greeting banner** ("Good afternoon! Welcome to…") just below the navbar
- **Video gallery** — if the Owner/Manager pastes a YouTube, Vimeo, or direct video URL into **Videos** in the dashboard, it shows up embedded (responsive 16:9 player) in a "Watch" section on the public site; the section (and its nav link) only appears once at least one video is added
- **Step-by-step booking wizard**: Branch → Service (with category filter) → Stylist (with live "Available/Not Available" status and working-day tags, or "Anyone") → a real calendar with a live availability grid per time slot → your details → **payment method** → a review-and-confirm summary — ending in a polished confirmation screen with your booking reference. Ghana phone number validation throughout (`0XXXXXXXXX` or `+233XXXXXXXXX`)
- **Pay-at-booking (optional)** — at checkout the customer picks from whichever payment methods the Owner has switched on in Settings (Cash at Front Desk, MTN MoMo, Vodafone Cash, Telecel Cash, AirtelTigo Money, Bank Transfer — see **Accepted Payment Methods** below). Cash needs nothing further; any mobile money/bank option shows the Owner's number/name or bank details with a one-tap **Copy** button next to the number, and requires the customer to upload a screenshot of their payment as proof before they can confirm. **Each mobile money network has its own number** (MTN MoMo, Vodafone Cash, Telecel Cash, and AirtelTigo Money are genuinely different wallets in Ghana — a customer can't send Vodafone Cash to an MTN number), and a method only ever shows to customers once the Owner has actually filled in its number/account — an unconfigured network or bank account is automatically hidden even if switched on, so no one is offered a way to pay that doesn't actually work. This is a **manual verification workflow, not a payment gateway** — no card processor or MoMo API is integrated, nothing is charged automatically, and no payment credentials are collected by this app; the admin reviews the screenshot and clicks **Verify Payment** in Appointments once satisfied it's genuine
- SMS/Email confirmation the moment a booking is submitted
- **"Send Us a Message" contact form** on the public site — emails straight to the business's Contact Email (via `MailApp`), no admin login needed
- Customer reviews display **plus a public "Leave a Review" star-rating form**
- **"My Bookings"** — search by the phone number you booked with, *or* by your booking reference code; results show as a **Kanban board grouped by status** (Pending/Confirmed/Completed/No-show/Cancelled), with a Cancel button on any upcoming booking
- Contact section: click-to-call, WhatsApp deep link, embedded Google Map, a full **weekly working-hours table** (per-day open/close times, 24-hour days, or closed), **social media icon row** (only shows platforms the Owner has filled in). Single-shop mode shows one global weekly schedule (set in Settings → Booking); multi-branch mode shows each branch's own hours next to a live open/closed dot, since different locations can genuinely keep different hours (edit each branch's schedule from its own Edit button on the Branches page)
- A live **Open Now / Closed Now** badge next to the business name in the navbar — computed server-side in Africa/Accra time, so it's accurate regardless of the visitor's own timezone. Only shows when there's one unambiguous schedule to report (single-shop mode, or exactly one branch); the same badge appears in the admin topbar for every role, so staff always know at a glance whether the shop is currently open
- A floating **WhatsApp button** (pre-filled "I'd like to book an appointment" message) and a floating **chat-bubble FAQ assistant** that answers from your live services/prices/hours/contact data — a fast rule-based assistant, not a paid generative AI, so it works with no API key or ongoing cost
- A small hand-built inline-SVG icon set throughout (no emoji, no icon-font download)
- **Visual refresh**: bolder/larger heading scale, an accent-underlined section-title treatment, deeper card shadows with a lift-on-hover on service/staff/review cards, a bigger and more legible hero with a stronger text-shadow and underline-on-hover nav links, a more prominent footer with an accent top border, and smooth-scrolling anchor navigation — aimed at reading as a modern, professional small-business site rather than a bare form-driven page

`Staff.WorkDays` is a comma-separated list of weekday abbreviations (e.g. `Mon,Tue,Wed,Thu,Fri,Sat`) that drives each stylist's permanent "Available/Not Available" status and the calendar in the public booking wizard; a row in `StaffLeave` adds a one-off exception on top of that — a specific date that one stylist is out, without touching their regular schedule. Weekly hours use the same JSON shape (`{ Sun: { open, is24, start, end }, Mon: {...}, … }`) in two different places depending on `Settings.HasBranches`: **single-shop mode** (`HasBranches='N'`) uses the one global `Settings.WeeklyHours`, edited in Settings → Booking; **multi-branch mode** uses each `Branches.WeeklyHours` instead, edited from that branch's own Edit button — since different locations can run different hours. Either way it's the single source of truth for the booking wizard's time slots, the Manage Slots grid, the public "Working Hours" display, and the live Open Now/Closed Now badge. `Branches.OpeningHours` is never typed by hand anymore — it's auto-derived from `WeeklyHours` (e.g. "Mon-Sat 8:00 AM – 7:00 PM, Sun 12:00 PM – 5:00 PM") the moment a branch is saved, so there's only one place that can go out of sync. The older flat `Settings.BookingStartHour`/`BookingEndHour` keys still exist as a one-time fallback if `WeeklyHours` is ever missing/corrupt, but are no longer shown in the UI. `Settings` also stores `SlotIntervalMinutes`, `HasBranches`, `ActivePaymentMethods` (comma-separated subset of the six payment methods actually offered at checkout), `SocialFacebook`/`SocialInstagram`/`SocialTwitter`/`SocialTiktok`/`SocialYoutube`, `FontFamily` (the site font), and a separate number/account-name pair per payment method — `MomoNumber`/`MomoName` (MTN MoMo), `VodafoneCashNumber`/`VodafoneCashName`, `TelecelCashNumber`/`TelecelCashName`, `AirtelTigoMoneyNumber`/`AirtelTigoMoneyName`, and `BankName`/`BankAccountName`/`BankAccountNumber`. `Users.FullName` is captured so an Owner can tell who a pending self-registered account actually belongs to before approving it, and now doubles as the name used in the admin greeting banner. A row in `BlockedSlots` closes one time slot, branch-wide, on one date — managed from the **Manage Slots** admin page. `Appointments.PaymentMethod` is one of `Cash`/`MTN MoMo`/`Vodafone Cash`/`Telecel Cash`/`AirtelTigo Money`/`Bank Transfer`; `PaymentStatus` is `Pay at Shop` (cash), `Awaiting Payment` (chose to pay now but hasn't uploaded proof yet), `Pending Verification` (proof uploaded, awaiting admin review), or `Paid` (admin verified it); `PaymentProofURL` is the customer's uploaded screenshot, stored in the same Drive folder as other site images. A row in `Visits` is one visitor-day (deduped by `VisitorKey` + `Date`), used only for the Overview visitor counters.

The schema self-heals: if a sheet or column above is missing from an already-deployed spreadsheet (e.g. you update to a newer version of this app that added a column), the next page load automatically creates/fixes it — no manual re-run of `setupSheets()` needed.

Sample data (2 branches, 10 services, 5 staff with working days, 4 products, 5 customers, 8 reviews, 2 hero slides, 6 gallery photos) is seeded automatically on first run so the app is immediately demoable.

## Deployment

1. **Create the Google Sheet.** Go to [sheets.google.com](https://sheets.google.com), create a new blank spreadsheet (this will be your database).
2. **Open the Apps Script editor.** In the Sheet, go to `Extensions → Apps Script`.
3. **Add the code.**
   - Rename the default `Code.gs` file (or paste over it) with the contents of `Code.gs` from this repo.
   - Click the `+` next to Files → `HTML` → name it exactly `index` → paste in the contents of `index.html`.
4. **Run setup once.** In the Apps Script editor toolbar, select the `setupSheets` function from the function dropdown and click **Run**. Grant the requested permissions (Sheets, Drive, Gmail/MailApp, external requests for SMS). This creates every tab with headers and seeds sample data, including a default admin login (see below).
5. **Deploy as a Web App.**
   - Click **Deploy → New deployment**.
   - Select type **Web app**.
   - Set **Execute as**: `Me`.
   - Set **Who has access**: `Anyone` (so customers can reach the public booking page without logging in).
   - Click **Deploy**, authorize, and copy the **Web app URL** — this is your live site.
6. **(Optional) Update an existing deployment.** After making changes, use **Deploy → Manage deployments → Edit → New version** so the live URL picks up your changes.
7. **(Optional) Daily appointment reminders.** In the Apps Script editor, go to `Triggers` (clock icon) → `Add Trigger` → function `sendUpcomingAppointmentReminders`, time-driven, run daily (e.g. every day 8am–9am, Africa/Accra time zone) to automatically SMS/email customers about tomorrow's appointment.

## Notes on architecture
- Every price shown to a customer or used in a sale is resolved **server-side** from the `Services`/`Products` sheets — the client only ever sends IDs and quantities, preventing price tampering.
- All `google.script.run` calls go through a single `api()` wrapper with a visible loading bar and toast-based error handling — no silent failures.
- Sessions are stateless server tokens (`CacheService`, 6-hour TTL) — no passwords are ever kept in the browser beyond the login form submission.
- The whole front end is one HTML file with no external JS/CSS framework, keeping the public page light for customers on mobile data in Ghana.
- **Every sheet column is forced to Plain Text format** (`setNumberFormat('@')`) at setup time. Without this, Google Sheets silently "smart-formats" values that look like numbers or times as you write them — a phone number like `0247123456` loses its leading zero, and a time slot like `09:00` gets converted into an actual time value — which then breaks exact-match lookups (phone number search, slot-availability checks) elsewhere in the app.
- **Uploaded photos/logos are delivered as embedded image data, not hotlinked or fetched as a separate resource at all.** Four things were tried across earlier updates to get an uploaded photo to display reliably: three different Drive hotlink URL formats (`uc?export=view`, `drive.google.com/thumbnail`, `lh3.googleusercontent.com/d/...`), and then routing through this app's own `doGet(e)` as a `?img=<fileId>` link. Every one of them asks the browser to fetch the image as its own separate HTTP request — and it turns out an `<img>` tag inside this app's own HtmlService-sandboxed page, fetching from any of those (including the app's own address), runs into Google's redirect/consent handling for that flow unpredictably. That's why photos kept failing to preview — first just in Safari, then (once routed through the app's own `?img=` link) everywhere at once. The actual fix: stop asking the browser to fetch images as separate resources. Every endpoint that hands data to the browser (`getPublicData`, `getServices`, `getStaff`, `getProducts`, `getGallery`, `getHeroSlides`, `getSettings`, `updateSettings`) now converts each Drive-backed image reference into a `data:` URI — the real image bytes, read server-side and embedded directly in the same `google.script.run` response already carrying everything else that page needs (`imageUrlToDataUri_()`/`withImageDataUris_()`). That's the one delivery path already proven to work identically in every browser, because the rest of the app's data already relies on it — no external host, no redirect, no separate fetch for the browser to handle differently. This recognizes and converts image references saved under any of the three prior URL formats too, so already-uploaded photos display correctly without being re-uploaded — nothing to migrate, since the small reference stored in the sheet doesn't change, only how it's read. The one place that still needs a real, independently-fetchable link is HTML email (a mail client fetches images itself, with no `google.script.run` channel available) — the greeting/reminder emails' logo intentionally keeps using the raw stored URL for that reason, via `doGet`'s `?img=` handler, which remains in place as a plain top-level "open this file" link (also used for the admin's "View Proof" payment-screenshot link, which needs the same thing).
- **The upload folder is created up front, not lazily.** `SalonSystem_Uploads` — the one Drive folder every uploaded photo lands in (logo, staff, services, products, gallery, hero slides, payment proofs) — is now created during `setupSheets()` itself rather than waiting for the first upload to trigger it. Dashboard → Branding & Theme has a new "View Uploads Folder in Drive" link next to the logo picker for the Owner to open it directly.
- **`SETUP_VERSION` — a durable, versioned migration gate.** Earlier, the Plain Text fix above (and any future one like it) only actually ran against a spreadsheet whose sheet *headers* didn't already match — so on a spreadsheet created before the fix existed, it silently never took effect even after redeploying newer code, because the headers already matched and the check short-circuited. `ensureSetup_()` now also compares a `setupVersion` stored in `PropertiesService` (which survives forever, unlike the 1-hour `CacheService` flag) against the in-code `SETUP_VERSION` constant, and forces `setupSheets()` to run at least once whenever it's behind — guaranteeing every such fix actually reaches every existing spreadsheet, not just brand-new ones. Bump `SETUP_VERSION` any time you add a fix here that needs to be re-applied to already-deployed spreadsheets.
- **One-time data repair on that same migration**: `repairPhoneColumns_()` restores a phone number that lost its leading zero to a 9-digit number before the Plain Text fix took effect (any 9-digit value in a Phone column is unambiguously missing that zero), and `repairTimeSlotColumns_()` fixes a `TimeSlot` value that got silently stored as a Time value instead of a plain "HH:mm" string (the same root cause — a value that *looks* like a time gets auto-formatted before Plain Text is in effect). The latter was the actual cause of two separately-reported symptoms: a slot closed in **Manage Slots** not staying visually marked closed after a reload, and (in principle) availability math being thrown off — because the stored TimeSlot could no longer exact-match the "HH:mm" keys the rest of the app looks it up by. Both repairs are idempotent and run automatically on the next page load after this update; they can't recover a booking's *other* fields, only its Phone/TimeSlot values.
- The one-time schema self-heal check (`ensureSetup_`, described above) is cached for an hour via `CacheService` rather than running on every page load — the earlier version re-verified all 13+ sheet headers on every single request, which was the single biggest cause of the app feeling slow to open.
- **`updateSettings()` now reads and writes the Settings sheet once, not once per key.** Saving ~30 settings at once used to mean ~30+ separate `getValues()`/`setValues()` round trips (one per key, via the same generic `findRowIndexById_`/`updateById_` helpers every other sheet uses) — that serial chain of small Sheets API calls was the actual cause of "Save All Settings" feeling slow. It now reads the whole (small) sheet once, updates everything in memory, and writes it back in one or two calls total.
- **Manage Slots trusts the toggle call's own response, not a re-fetch.** Toggling a slot used to call `toggleBlockedSlot` and then immediately re-fetch the whole day's grid with `getSlotsForAdmin` to reconcile the UI. That extra read was removed — the mutation's own response already says exactly what was just written, so using it directly is both faster (one round trip instead of two) and immune to a slot ever appearing to silently revert to open after a click.
- **One weekly-hours source of truth drives everything time-related.** `getEffectiveWeeklyHours_()` picks `Settings.WeeklyHours` (single-shop mode) or that branch's own `Branches.WeeklyHours` (multi-branch mode), and `getAvailableSlots()` (public booking) and `getSlotsForAdmin()` (Manage Slots) both derive that date's start/end minute window — and whether the day is bookable at all — from it, instead of a flat all-week start/end hour. A day marked closed is gated out before staff schedules (or a stylist's `StaffLeave`) are even considered, both in what's offered and in `createAppointment()`'s own server-side re-validation (`isSlotAvailable_`), so the public wizard, Manage Slots, and the actual booking guard can never disagree about whether a given day is open.
- **Heads up if you're updating from an earlier version with existing branch rows**: `Branches.WeeklyHours` is a brand-new column, so it starts empty on any branch that existed before this update — until you open that branch (Branches page → Edit) and hit Save once, `getEffectiveWeeklyHours_()` falls back to a generic "every day 9am–6pm" default for its slot generation, even though its `OpeningHours` display text still shows whatever it said before. Re-saving each existing branch through the new Working Hours editor (even with no changes) is what actually sets its real schedule.
- Apps Script + Google Sheets is a real, live database, not an in-memory cache — every save and most page loads involve at least one live round trip to Sheets, so some latency (typically under a couple of seconds) is inherent to this platform and can't be fully eliminated. Buttons across the app now give immediate visual feedback (disabled + "Saving…" state, optimistic UI on Manage Slots, confirmation toasts) so actions register clearly even while that round trip is in flight.
- **Stale-while-revalidate (SWR) caching on the public site.** The very first thing a visitor's browser does used to be wait on a live `getPublicData` round trip before anything appeared. Now, if a copy of that data is sitting in `localStorage` from the last 5 minutes, the whole public site (services, staff, gallery, videos, reviews, hero, theme…) paints from it **instantly**, while a fresh copy is fetched silently in the background and swapped in — without resetting the branch selector, hero slider, or an in-progress booking wizard if the visitor has already started one. This is scoped to the public site's initial load, the single biggest lever for perceived speed since every visitor hits it; it is not (yet) applied to every admin list.
- **Single-shop mode now hides branch dropdowns everywhere, not just on the public site.** `Settings.HasBranches` was always the master toggle — `'Y'` means the Owner manages multiple physical locations and can add/edit/delete `Branches` rows from the Branches admin page; `'N'` means there's exactly one shop and that whole page is about editing *its* details, not a list to manage. Previously the "one shop" half of that promise wasn't fully honored: every admin modal with a branch field (Appointments, Services, Staff, Products, Expenses, Gallery, Manage Slots) still rendered a `<select>` with one option, and the Point-of-Sale top-right branch switcher could be left unset, which made checkout dead-end on "Select a branch in the top-right switcher before checking out a sale" — a switcher the admin never had a reason to touch because there was nothing to switch between. A shared `branchArg()`/`showBranchPicker_()`/`branchSelectHtml()`/`branchSelectValue()` helper set now makes this automatic everywhere: with only one branch on record, no picker is rendered anywhere, every branch-scoped save silently uses that one branch's ID, and POS checkout resolves it the same way instead of relying on the switcher having been touched. The moment a second branch is added, every one of those dropdowns reappears on its own — nothing to configure separately.
- **Service Categories are now admin-managed, not free-typed per service.** Dashboard → Services previously let each service's "Category" be typed by hand, so "Haircut" and "Haircuts" (or a stray trailing space) would silently split what should have been one category in the public site's service filter. The Services admin page now opens with a "Service Categories" panel — an editable chip list stored in `Settings.ServiceCategories` (comma-separated) — and the Add/Edit Service modal offers a `<select>` populated from that list instead of a text box. Removing a category from the list doesn't touch services already saved under it; an existing service's category is appended to the dropdown automatically if it's ever out of sync with the current list, so old data is never silently orphaned or blanked.
- **Public site sections can be switched off from Settings.** Dashboard → Branding & Theme now has two toggles — "Greeting banner" and "Our Team section" (`Settings.ShowGreetingBanner`/`ShowTeamSection`, default `'Y'`) — for Owners who don't want a personalized greeting strip or a staff-photos section on their public page. Turning "Our Team" off also removes its nav link, so there's no dead link pointing at a hidden section.
- **The "Open Now"/"Closed Now" badge is styled as a proper status tag with a live pulse.** It's a pill with a colored dot instead of plain text — green with a soft pulsing ring animation when open (so it visibly reads as "live," not static text), red and still when closed.
- **The public site has its own reload button**, next to the mobile menu icon in the top nav (the admin dashboard already had one). It re-fetches `getPublicData` and gives a brief spin + confirmation toast, for a customer who wants to double-check live availability/pricing without a full page refresh.
- **The admin notifications dropdown no longer overflows off-screen on narrow phones.** It's positioned with `right: 0` relative to the bell button itself, which works fine on desktop but, on a narrow viewport where the bell sits close to the screen's left edge, a fixed 300px-wide panel growing leftward from that anchor could run out of screen — clipping its own content off the left edge instead of shifting to fit. Below 860px wide it now anchors to the viewport instead of the bell button (`position: fixed` with `left`/`right` insets, matching the same top offset already used by the mobile help-chat panel in this topbar), so it always fits fully on screen regardless of exactly where the bell button lands in the topbar's wrapped layout.
